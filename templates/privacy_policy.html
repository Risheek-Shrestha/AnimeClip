What we collect

We collect the information you give us directly when you create an account (username, email address, age) and information generated by using the service: watch history, ratings, comments, playlists, and search activity. This is used to run core features like Continue Watching and personalised recommendations.

How we use it

Account and activity data is used to operate the service: authenticating you, remembering playback progress, generating recommendations, and sending account-related email (such as password resets and email verification). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services

Video and image assets are delivered via Cloudinary. Error reports may be sent to Sentry if configured. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for login sessions and CSRF protection. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data retention & deletion

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Contact the site administrator to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Children's privacy

Accounts require a minimum age and Kids Mode sub-profiles restrict mature content; the service is not directed at collecting personal information from children beyond what's needed to enforce these age controls.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the site administrator.
